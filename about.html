About Roadside America

The Roadside America Project is a unique body of editorial work and collaboration that began with books in published 1986 and 1992 and later expanded to include a popular website and mobile apps. The Project currently documents and writes about over 20,000 points-of-interest in the United States and Canada.

Who's behind it

Since the publication of the original book -- Roadside America* -- in 1985, writers and road trip know-it-alls Doug Kirby, Ken Smith, Mike Wilkins have visited, documented and photographed their backroad discoveries. Their work has appeared online since 1994 (RoadsideAmerica.com since 1996), as well as in their iPhone applications since 2010. The Roadside America experts are aided by a horde of travelers, shoving little pushpins of peculiarity into the landscape for all to enjoy. Members of the original team continue to write, edit and hand-curate every piece of content. Susan Kirby (who designed the first book) is the business and marketing lead.
